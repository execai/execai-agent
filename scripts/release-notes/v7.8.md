## What's new

**7.8 lets subagents work as long as the task needs, and you can give them more steps on the go.** The VS Code extension 0.2.37 and ExecAI Studio 0.1.48 update together with the agent.

### Subagents no longer stop after five minutes

Every subagent used to be cut off after exactly five minutes, even in the
middle of its work, and one waiting for a free slot lost that time too.
Now a subagent works until it finishes or runs out of steps.

- A safety limit of 2 hours stays, only against a run that hangs. The
  clock starts when the subagent actually starts: time in the queue does
  not count.
- Change it with `EXECAI_SUBAGENT_TIMEOUT`: `90m`, `3h`, or `0` for no
  limit. If the limit is ever hit, the message says how to change it.

### More steps, and you can see how many are left

- A subagent gets **50 steps** by default (it was 15) and can start with
  up to **100** (it was 50).
- Every working subagent shows how far it is: "step 12/50 · 38 left" in
  the **Agents** block in the terminal, in the editor panel (VS Code,
  Cursor, ExecAI Studio) and in `execai ui`. With 5 steps or fewer left,
  the line turns yellow.
- The default for new subagents: `/agents limit 80` in the terminal or
  **Subagent steps** in the panel menu. It is saved in the config.

### Give a subagent more steps on the go

- **You.** **+25** next to a working subagent in the panel, or
  `/agents more <n> [steps]` in the terminal: the limit grows at once and
  the subagent keeps going.
- **Continue where it stopped.** A subagent that ran out of steps is
  marked "hit the step limit". **Continue (+25)** in the panel (or
  `/agents more`) picks it up from the same place, in the same transcript,
  and its result comes back to the chat when it is done.
- **The main agent.** The new `TaskExtend` tool: when a subagent stops at
  its limit, the main agent sees it and decides whether to give it more
  steps or to restate the task. It can extend only its own subagents.
- However often it is extended, one subagent gets at most 500 steps in
  total.

### Fixes

- A subagent that spent all its steps on tools without writing a word was
  reported as an error and could not be continued. Now it is marked as
  having hit the step limit and can be continued like any other.
