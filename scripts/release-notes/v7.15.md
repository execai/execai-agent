## What's new

**7.15 shows what is already set up on your machine and what is left, and lets you call it from your phone.**

### Getting started on the first launch

The first launch now opens with five steps instead of a greeting: sign in,
connect models, turn on sync, bind a folder to a web project, call the
agent from your phone. Each step is marked done or not by the real state
of the machine, with one line on why it matters and the command to type.

- `/start` shows the steps again at any time, with "N of 5 done" on top.
- When you are not signed in yet, the steps come before the sign-in link.
- The sync step says what travels (the config with your provider keys,
  your personal memory, the files of bound projects — all encrypted on
  your computer with your sync password), what stays on this machine, and
  that the sync password cannot be recovered.

### `/machine` — the "My machine" card

One card with everything about this computer:

- OS, agent version, the account and the plan;
- every model source and whether it answers, with the time of the check;
  `/machine check` asks them all again;
- sync: on or off, the last upload, what is inside;
- whether the machine takes tasks (`execai serve` or an open editor
  window) and can be called from the web chat and the phone — and if not,
  what is missing.

### `/phone` — call this machine from your phone

`/phone` draws a QR code in the terminal and prints the same link. Point
the phone camera at it: the web chat opens with this machine chosen for
tasks.

- Tasks from the web chat and the phone come with the Work and Max plans
  and need `execai serve` (or an open editor window) on the machine.
- They work on your own provider keys too.

### For the editor panel

`execai ide` has a new `machine_state` command. The editor panel and
`execai ui` get the machine card, the getting-started steps and the phone
link in one event, and the agent sends it again by itself when something
changes: sign-in or sign-out, a source connected or disconnected, folder
bindings, sync, `execai serve` started or stopped. An older panel simply
skips it.

The panel in `execai ui` already has **Getting started** and
**My machine** in its menu, with the QR code behind a button.

### Fixes

- In the default terminal mode the lines printed at startup — the greeting
  and the sign-in prompt among them — never reached the screen. They are
  shown again, so the sign-in link is visible on the first launch.
